# Rizgary Regrade — reader worklist

Generated by `make_grading_worklist.py`. Chapter 3 `sec:method-report-verification` level 2 specifies two qualified readers independently assigning Normal/Mild, Moderate or Severe at L1-L2 through L5-S1, blinded to model predictions and, where feasible, to the routine report on the first pass.

## Why a regrade is needed

The routine reports give a usable *binary* signal but not a usable *graded* one. Across 195 reports only 5 state "moderate" and 9 state "severe" unambiguously, and only 54 attribute canal stenosis to a named level, leaving 84% of case-by-level cells unstated. A three-class transfer result computed on that many graded positives would carry a confidence interval spanning most of its range.

## Two sheets, and why they are separate

| File | Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| `grading_sheet_blinded.csv` | **First pass.** Case id and level only, with an empty grade column. No extracted label, no report text, no folder name. |
| `adjudication_sheet.csv` | **Second pass and disagreements only.** Carries the extracted status, severity wording, source phrase and imaging path. |

Handing the adjudication sheet to a first-pass reader would defeat the blinding Chapter 3 asks for, which is why they are written separately rather than as one annotated file.

Note that the morphology folder a case sits in (`normal`, `disc bulge`, ...) is itself a label and is deliberately omitted from the blinded sheet.

## Priority order

Every case listed has imaging. If the whole set cannot be graded, work down the priorities — level 3 of the same section permits a stratified subset.

| Priority | What | Cases | Why first |
| ---: | :--- | ---: | :--- |
| **1** | Ambiguous, silent, or spreadsheet/report disagreement | 25 | These cannot be resolved from text at all. They need an image read regardless of what else is done. |
| **2** | Report asserts stenosis | 50 | The scarce class. Every graded positive materially widens what can be claimed. |
| **3** | Explicit negatives, sampled | 40 | Needed to estimate specificity; a positives-only set cannot. |

## Grading instructions

1. Grade **central canal stenosis only**. Chapter 3 `sec:method-schema-alignment` excludes foraminal targets unless laterality and grade are adequate, and subarticular entirely, because the routine reports do not support them.
2. Use the same three grades as the RSNA target: **Normal/Mild**, **Moderate**, **Severe**. Normal and Mild are one class in the source task, so a mild finding is Normal/Mild — this is the boundary that makes the two cohorts comparable.
3. Grade every level L1-L2 through L5-S1, including the ones the report never mentioned. Recovering those cells is much of the point.
4. If a level cannot be assessed, mark `not_assessable` rather than guessing a grade.
5. Do not consult the routine report on the first pass.

Two readers grade independently; disagreements go to a senior reader. Weighted kappa, exact agreement and the split of one-grade versus two-grade disagreements are reported, per Chapter 3.
