# Literature Review Starter

Begin with these parts of Selar’s Chapter 2:

- Clinical background and the three anatomical forms of stenosis.
- MRI acquisition, sequences, planes, and diagnostic yield.
- Clinical grading systems and inter-observer reliability.
- Datasets and benchmarks, for comparison with published cohorts.
- Evaluation and generalisation, especially sampling and reporting limitations.

The student should add regional studies, epidemiology methods, GEE/mixed-effects references, STROBE guidance, and papers using comparable finding definitions. Do not copy Selar’s PhD novelty claims into this epidemiological project.
