# MSc 1 — Lumbar MRI Cohort Characterisation (Candidate: Elaf)

**Primary MSc Candidate:** Elaf  
**Academic Supervisor:** Dr. Polla Abdulhamid Fattah  

## Purpose

Describe level-specific lumbar degenerative findings among patients who underwent MRI at Rizgary Teaching Hospital, and examine patterns by age and sex.

This is a retrospective tertiary-hospital referral cohort. It is not a population-prevalence study.
