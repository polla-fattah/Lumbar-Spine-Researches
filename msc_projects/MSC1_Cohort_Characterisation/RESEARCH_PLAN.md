# Research Plan

## Working title

Level-Specific Distribution and Demographic Patterns of Lumbar Degenerative MRI Findings in a Tertiary-Hospital Cohort in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq

## Research questions

1. What are the observed frequencies and confidence intervals of major findings at L1–L2 through L5–S1?
2. How do findings vary by age and sex?
3. Which levels show the greatest observed burden?
4. Which age/sex associations remain after accounting for repeated levels within patients?
5. How does this cohort compare descriptively with published cohorts, given differences in sampling and definitions?

## Planned findings

Bulge, protrusion, extrusion, central canal stenosis, foraminal narrowing where explicitly reported, nerve-root pressure, facet arthrosis, ligamentum flavum hypertrophy, and osteophytes where consistently extractable. Subarticular stenosis is excluded unless new reliable annotation is obtained.

## Analysis

- Descriptive patient and level summaries with 95% confidence intervals.
- Age-band and sex stratification.
- Co-occurrence and level heatmaps.
- GEE or mixed-effects logistic regression with patient-level clustering.
- Pre-specified primary outcomes and multiplicity control for secondary analyses.

## Deliverables

Dissertation, codebook, audited structured dataset, reproducible analysis code, tables/figures, and a manuscript prepared for submission.

## Extension space

Possible extensions include reader agreement, reporting completeness, level-specific co-occurrence networks, or comparison with another regional cohort. Any extension must be added to `CHANGELOG.md` before analysis.
