# Conditional Research Plan

## Working title

Association Between Lumbar MRI Degenerative Findings, Presenting Symptoms, Treatment Path, and Outcome in a Middle Eastern Clinical Cohort

## Activation conditions

- Written permission for imaging–clinical linkage.
- Reliable symptom and treatment variables.
- Defined follow-up outcome and time point.
- Cohort flow and missingness audit.

## Research questions

1. How do level-resolved MRI findings relate to symptom patterns?
2. Which findings are associated with surgery versus conservative treatment after adjustment?
3. Does a pre-specified multilevel burden score improve discrimination over the worst single grade?
4. If follow-up is adequate, which baseline variables associate with treatment response?

## Method

Use transparent association/prediction models: logistic, ordinal, multinomial, or penalised regression as appropriate. Compare clinical-only, imaging-only, and combined models only if sample size supports it. Do not make causal claims.

If follow-up is inadequate, narrow the project to cross-sectional imaging–symptom association.
