# Activation and Governance Checklist

- [ ] Institutional authority permits imaging–symptom–treatment linkage.
- [ ] Linkage key is restricted and separately stored.
- [ ] Symptoms and symptom duration available.
- [ ] Examination findings availability assessed.
- [ ] Treatment pathway and procedure data available.
- [ ] Follow-up completeness assessed.
- [ ] Primary outcome and time point defined before modelling.
- [ ] Missingness and exclusions documented.
- [ ] No synthetic outcomes or simulated patients used as a substitute.
